Services

Assessment services built for technical rigor and regulatory readiness.

Every engagement follows ISO 14040/14044 methodology, so results hold up under third-party critical review, customer audits, and evolving disclosure regulations.

Life Cycle Assessment

Cradle-to-grave or cradle-to-gate modeling, done to ISO 14040/44.

We quantify impacts across carbon, water, and resource categories — scoped correctly the first time, so the results hold up to internal and external review.

Environmental Product Declarations

Program-operator-ready EPDs, from LCA data to publication.

We turn your underlying LCA into a verifiable, publishable disclosure document formatted for your target program operator — UL, NSF, SCS, ASTM, or others.

Carbon Footprinting

GHG Protocol-aligned carbon accounting, product and corporate.

Operational and embodied carbon inventories benchmarked against science-based targets, built to support CDP, SASB, and GRI disclosures.

ESG Advisory

Materiality assessments and reporting support.

We help you identify what actually matters to your stakeholders and build reporting that holds up under CSRD, GRI, and SASB frameworks.
