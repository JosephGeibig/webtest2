Third-party confidence in every number you publish.

What we verify

Review services matched to how the study will be used.

The right level of scrutiny depends on whether a study supports an internal decision, a public claim, or a regulatory disclosure.

Internal QA Review

A structured sanity check on a study still in development — model logic, data quality, and calculation checks before it goes external.

Independent Critical Review

A single-reviewer or panel critical review conducted to ISO 14044/14071, required for any study supporting a public comparative assertion.

EPD Verification

Verification against a specific program operator's requirements (e.g. UL, NSF, SCS, ASTM) so the EPD is accepted without rework.

Panel Review

A multi-reviewer panel for high-stakes or contested studies, coordinated end-to-end including reviewer selection and report reconciliation.

Our process

A consistent path from draft study to signed-off report.

Every review follows the same structure, regardless of scope — so you know what to expect and when.

01

Document Review

Check goal, scope, and system boundary against the study's stated purpose.

02

Data Traceability

Trace inventory data back to source documentation and flag unsupported assumptions.

03

Methodology Conformance

Confirm the model follows ISO 14040/14044 and any program-specific rules that apply.

04

Reviewer Report & Sign-off

Deliver a formal reviewer statement addressing every finding, ready for publication.

Under the hood

What a critical review actually checks.

System boundary consistency

Confirms the boundary matches the functional unit and hasn't shifted between stages of the study.

Data quality and representativeness

Evaluates whether primary vs. secondary data sources are appropriate for the claims being made.

Allocation and cut-off rules

Checks that co-product allocation and cut-off decisions are applied consistently and are defensible.

Impact assessment method selection

Confirms the characterization method fits the goal of the study and is applied correctly.

Interpretation and reported claims

Verifies that conclusions and any public-facing claims are actually supported by the results.
