Industries

Sustainability expertise across the value chain.

Every industry answers to different standards and different customers. We've built LCA and carbon programs across a wide range of sectors, and we work within the frameworks each one requires.

Where we serve

Sector experience that shapes how we scope your study.

Building Products & Materials

Cradle-to-gate LCAs and EPDs for materials manufacturers pursuing LEED, Buy Clean, and procurement requirements.

Consumer Packaged Goods

Product footprints and packaging assessments for brands facing retailer and regulatory disclosure pressure.

Industrial Manufacturing

Facility-level and product-level carbon accounting for manufacturers with complex, multi-tier supply chains.

Chemicals & Materials Science

LCA and material health assessments for specialty chemical producers and materials innovators.

Furniture & Textiles

Product transparency and EPD support for the furnishings and textile supply chain, including Cradle to Cradle.

Electronics & Technology

Embodied carbon and end-of-life impact modeling for hardware manufacturers and data infrastructure providers.
