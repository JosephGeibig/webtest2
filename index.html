Our process

A methodology that mirrors the product's own life cycle.

We scope the system boundary first, then move stage by stage — so nothing upstream or downstream of your factory gate gets missed.

01

Goal & Scope

Define functional unit, system boundary, and assessment purpose.

02

Inventory Analysis

Collect primary and secondary data across every input and output.

03

Impact Assessment

Translate inventory data into climate, water, and resource metrics.

04

Interpretation

Identify hotspots and reduction levers specific to your product.

05

FAQ

What's the difference between an LCA and an EPD?

An LCA is the underlying study; an EPD is the standardized document that publishes its results.

FAQ

How long does an assessment take?

Most single-product LCAs run 8–12 weeks from data collection to final report, depending on supply chain complexity.
