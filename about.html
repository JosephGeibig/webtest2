About Us

Consultants first. Practitioners second only to that.

[Company Name] was founded on a simple idea: environmental claims should be able to survive scrutiny. We help manufacturers and brands build that rigor in from the start.

Why we exist

Sustainability claims are only as good as the study behind them.

Too many environmental claims are built on assumptions rather than measured data — and it shows the moment a customer, auditor, or regulator asks a follow-up question. We started [Company Name] to close that gap: rigorous, ISO-aligned assessment work that holds up when it's tested.

That means being honest about uncertainty, transparent about methodology, and willing to tell a client when their product's numbers don't support the claim they want to make.

How we serve

Two ways to work with us, depending on how much you already have in-house.

Some clients need a single study delivered well. Others need an ongoing partner who acts as their sustainability team. We do both.

Project-Based

A specific study, delivered end to end

You know what you need — an LCA, an EPD, a carbon footprint — and we scope, model, and deliver it on a defined timeline, ready for internal use or external publication.

Ongoing Partnership

An extension of your sustainability team

We take on a recurring role — managing your EPD program, running annual carbon inventories, or serving as your named LCA practitioner across a growing product line.
